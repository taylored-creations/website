What's coming

Three things, done right.

Everything on this channel falls into one of three buckets. If it doesn't help you build something better or buy something smarter, it doesn't get made.

Shop Buildout Series

A multi-part video series following the shop from bare walls to fully dialed: layout and zoning, lighting, power, dust collection, compressed air, storage, and custom workstations — with plans and cut lists where it makes sense.

Multi-episode

Non-Biased Reviews

Tools and shop gear tested in real work, not on a showroom table. I tell you what I paid, how I got it, what I liked, what annoyed me, and whether I'd buy it again — with long-term follow-ups when the honeymoon wears off.

Zero paid placement

DIY Projects

The occasional build for the home, the shop, or the property — furniture, fixtures, jigs, and fixes. Approachable projects with clear steps, honest time estimates, and the lessons learned the hard way.
